# G-DOC test fixtures: provenance

**Test-only.** Deleted with `src/document/testing/` at the P1-A integration step (G0 next-gate brief).

These are seven scene-v1 files from the regression corpus, copied byte for byte. They were generated by the `regression_corpus` example at `main` 29432da (the approved G-0 baseline), output directory `tests/output/g0c-regression/`, and are used here as fixture packet sources: the test-only encoder (`../synthetic.ts`, `encodeScene`) turns each one into a C3 packet.

- **Only procedural cases.** Their inputs are the procedural images under `tests/fixtures/procedural`. Nothing derived from the photographic inputs (`concert-*`, `architecture-*`, `portrait-*`, `texture-*`, `hokusai-*`, `talbot-*`) is copied.
- **Coverage:** bw (`circle-bw`, and `nested_rings-bw` for compound paths with holes), colour, mosaic, limited, halftone (artboard rect clip, background colour) and halftone-transparent. Even-odd fills appear in the bw, colour, mosaic and limited cases; non-zero fills in the halftone cases.
- **Extension.** Files carry a `.raw` suffix so the repository formatter (`vp check`, which formats `*.json`) leaves the bytes untouched. Strip `.raw` to get the corpus file name.
- **Integrity.** Each SHA-256 below was computed on the copy and checked against its entry in `docs/research/p0-r-geo/artifacts/regression/a4cf172.sha256`. All seven match.
